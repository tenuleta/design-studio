Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Netsanet Mekonen — founder & lead designer

What started as a one-person freelance practice has grown into Sunny Smile Printing PLC — a focused creative studio helping businesses look the way they deserve to look.

Over the last 8 years I've designed logos, identities, posters, flyers, and packaging for more than 120 projects across Ethiopia and beyond. My rule is simple: design should do a job — get attention, build trust, and win customers.

When I'm not pushing pixels, I'm mentoring young designers and speaking at creative meetups around Addis Ababa.
